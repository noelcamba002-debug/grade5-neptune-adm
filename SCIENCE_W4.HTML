LLEARNING EXPERIENCESDay 1 · Concept

Viviparous vs. Oviparous

Viviparous Animals: Give birth to live young. The baby develops inside the mother's body. Example: Dogs, Cats, Humans.

Oviparous Animals: Lay eggs, and the baby develops outside the mother's body. Example: Birds, Reptiles, Fish.

LLEARNING EXPERIENCESDay 2 · Advantages

Advantages of Reproduction

Viviparous: The embryo gets enough nourishment inside the mother's body to survive.

Oviparous: Animals can produce many young at once. The protective shell prevents the embryo from dying.

LLEARNING EXPERIENCESDay 3 · Animal Groups

Mammals, Birds, and Reptiles

Mammals: Give birth to live young, have hair/fur, and produce milk. (Dog, Cat, Cow)

Birds: Lay eggs, have feathers, wings, and beaks. (Chicken, Duck, Eagle)

Reptiles: Many lay eggs (some give birth), have dry scales. (Snake, Turtle, Lizard)

LLEARNING EXPERIENCESDay 4 · Comparison

Comparison of Animal Groups

Mammals: Live birth (Viviparous)

Birds: Lay eggs (Oviparous)

Reptiles: Mostly lay eggs (Oviparous), but some give birth.

Importance: Reproduction ensures that animal species continue to exist and maintain balance in nature.
