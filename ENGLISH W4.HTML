LLEARNING EXPERIENCESDay 2 · Concept

Possessive Nouns

Singular Possessive (ONE): Add 's. teacher → teacher's

Plural Possessive (MORE THAN ONE):

  • If it ends in s, add only '. (students → students')
  • If it does NOT end in s, add 's. (children → children's)

LLEARNING EXPERIENCESDay 3 · Concept

The Three Types of Compound Nouns

1. Closed

Written as ONE word.

Examples: playground, bedroom

2. Open

Written as TWO separate words.

Examples: school bus, birthday cake

3. Hyphenated

Joined by a HYPHEN (-).

Examples: mother-in-law, well-being
