LLEARNING EXPERIENCESDay 1 · Rules 2 & 5

Divisibility Rules for 2 and 5

Rule for 2: A number is divisible by 2 if its ones digit is 2, 4, 6, 8, or 0.

Rule for 5: A number is divisible by 5 if its ones digit is 0 or 5.

Examples:

  • 436 is divisible by 2 (ends in 6).
  • 225 is divisible by 5 (ends in 5).

Divisibility Rule for 10

Rule for 10: A number is divisible by 10 if its ones digit is 0.

Examples:

  • 720 is divisible by 10 (ends in 0).
  • 11,520 is divisible by 10 (ends in 0).

LLEARNING EXPERIENCESDay 3 · Rule for 3

Divisibility Rule for 3

A number is divisible by 3 if the sum of all its digits is divisible by 3.

Examples:

  • 540: 5 + 4 + 0 = 9 (divisible by 3).
  • 561: 5 + 6 + 1 = 12 (divisible by 3).

LLEARNING EXPERIENCESDay 3 · Rules 6 & 9

Rules for 6 and 9

Rule for 6: Divisible by both 2 and 3.

Rule for 9: Sum of digits is divisible by 9.

Examples:

  • 126: Even and 1+2+6=9 (divisible by 3). So divisible by 6.
  • 135: 1+3+5=9. Divisible by 9.
